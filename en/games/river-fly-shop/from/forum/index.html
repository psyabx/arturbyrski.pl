A calm fishing game about fly fishing, fly tying and running a fly shop

You run a small fly shop by the river. In the morning you go fishing, during the day you advise customers, and in the evening you sit down at the vise and tie the flies missing from the display case.

Customers ask what to fish with in specific conditions: the season, the weather, the water level and the insects hatching right now. Good advice builds the shop's reputation, and reputation opens up new waters.

The game is made for fly anglers, anglers who fish other methods and anyone who enjoys calm simulation games. The waters, fish and regulations are modelled on real Polish rivers, and the characters are inspired by real anglers.

River Fly Shop is a working title and may still change.

What already works in the test build

  • Shop and customers

    Customers come in asking what to use in given conditions. You pick flies from the display case, recommend and sell them, and at the end of the day you see the balance and how your reputation changed.

  • Vise and notebook

    You tie flies from recipes, step by step, using materials stored in drawers. The notebook keeps your patterns, notes from the river and trophies.

  • 3D fishing

    Five methods: dry fly with mending, short-line nymphing, dry-dropper, wet fly and streamer. With trees behind you, you make a roll cast. You wade the river and sneak, because fish can see the angler and their shadow.

  • Playing and landing

    While playing a fish you watch the tension so the tippet does not break, and steer the fish to the side. A tired fish goes into the net, and you take its photo for the notebook.

  • Two waters

    The Skawa at Witanowice is open from the start. The San, the Bachlawa stretch by the bench, opens at a higher reputation. You will find brown trout, grayling, chub and nase there, and in one spot, sometimes, a huchen.

  • Real rules and sound

    Closed seasons and size limits apply. Hints can be turned off so you only see what an angler would see. By the water you hear the river, birds that change with the time of day and season, wind and rain. The game is available in Polish and English.

Frequently asked questions

Is this game only for fly anglers?

No. It is made for fly anglers, but also for anglers who fish other methods and for players who enjoy calm simulation games. By the water you get hints that can be turned off, and a tutorial for people new to fly fishing is still in the works.

Which rivers can you fish and what can you catch?

The test build has two waters modelled on real rivers in Poland: the Skawa at Witanowice and the San on the Bachlawa stretch by the bench. They hold brown trout, grayling, chub and nase, and in one spot on the San, sometimes, a huchen.

Do you tie your own flies in the game?

Yes. At the vise you tie flies from recipes, step by step, using materials stored in drawers. There are five fishing methods: dry fly, short-line nymphing, dry-dropper, wet fly and streamer.

When and on what can I play?

The game is in development and has no release date yet. There is a playable test build for Windows PCs, currently tested by a small closed group. You can sign up for future playtests with one email.

Development log

What's new in the game, latest changes first. Log as RSS

22 November 2026

New methods, the roll cast and a livelier river

You can now fish a wet fly and a streamer, and tie a nymph on a dropper under a dry fly. With trees or a high bank behind you, you make a roll cast: the line does not travel backwards, it rolls out across the water. You can catch insects over the water with a net to see what is hatching. The river looks different: trees are mirrored in the water, the current breaks and foams behind boulders, and there is a new angler model. The game can be switched to English and the graphics quality set to match your computer.

21 November 2026

A second river: the San

Once the shop has a reputation, you can travel to the San, to the Bachlawa stretch by the bench: a run under the trees, small channels across from the bench, a hole under a tree and riffles. A huchen sometimes holds in the hole under the tree. It only takes a streamer and fights very hard. The game also got sound: the river, birds, wind and rain, plus calm music in the shop and at the vise.

20 November 2026

Shop sections and a vise with drawers

Shop stock is now sorted into sections, and customers ask more often about specific methods, such as wet flies or streamers. At the vise, materials sit in drawers, so it is quicker to find what a recipe needs.

19 November 2026

Short-line nymphing and conditions

A new method: short-line nymphing. You lead the rod tip after the nymph. Too fast and the nymph rises, too slow and slack hides the takes. Before going out you can also set the time of day, weather and water level.

18 November 2026

Fish photos and willows on the bank

After landing a fish you take its photo in the net, and the photo goes into your notebook. Willows now line the banks, and the camera stays close to the angler after the cast.

17 November 2026

The Skawa, wading and playing fish

The first water is the Skawa at Witanowice, with a real layout of the bottom and depths. You can step into the water and wade, and sneaking lets you get closer to fish. Playing and landing fish were added too.

16 November 2026

Daily rhythm and regulars

Each day has a rhythm: the river in the morning, the shop during the day, the vise in the evening. Regulars with their own stories visit the shop, and in the evening you see the day's balance. The notebook collects fly recipes and notes from the river.

12 November 2026

First playable build

The first playable build is here, with the three parts of the game: the shop, the vise and fishing one run of the river.
