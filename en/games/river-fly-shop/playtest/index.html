Sign up for the River Fly Shop playtest

I am looking for future playtesters: fly anglers, anglers and players who enjoy calm simulation games. You will need a Windows PC. Signing up takes one email.

  1. Click the button below. A new email with a ready subject line will open.
  2. If you like, answer two short questions in the message. They are optional.
  3. Send it. When playtesting starts, you will get an invitation at that address.
Send an email: sign me up

If the button does not open your email app, write to art.byrski@gmail.com with the subject “River Fly Shop – playtest”.

What happens to your address

  • Artur Byrski Link Technologies collects your email address and message only to invite you to the River Fly Shop playtest and to let you know about the release.
  • We do not add you to a newsletter, send ads or share your address with anyone.
  • We keep it until playtesting ends and the game is released. You can ask us to delete it at any time: just reply “delete”.
