Privacy: River Fly Shop playtest sign-ups

Effective from 22 November 2026.

Playtest sign-ups

  • Controller: Artur Byrski Link Technologies, ul. Kościelna 49, 34-100 Witanowice, Polska, VAT ID PL5512278698, email art.byrski@gmail.com.
  • What data: your email address and whatever you write in the message (e.g. whether you fly fish). Nothing is required apart from the address you write from.
  • Why: to invite you to the River Fly Shop playtest and to let you know when the game is released. We do not send newsletters or ads.
  • Legal basis: your consent, given by sending the email (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). You can withdraw it at any time by replying “delete”.
  • How long: until playtesting ends and the game is released, then we delete sign-ups. Sooner, if you ask.
  • Who has access: only us. Messages are kept in a Gmail mailbox, so technically Google stores them as the email provider. We never sell or share addresses.
  • Your rights: access, correction or deletion of your data, withdrawal of consent, and a complaint to the Polish data protection authority (Prezes UODO).

Visit statistics

The site counts visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics. It uses no cookies, does not store IP addresses and does not identify individual people. We only see totals, such as how many people visited the game page, which site they came from and how many times the sign-up page was opened. The tool cannot see the emails you send.

Changes

If this information changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new date.
